How to read the numbers — read this first

Parents often ask for “the RAN cutoff” or “the screened-group cutoff” for a school. Those numbers mostly do not exist in published form. Here is exactly what is — and is not — available, so the statistics below are not misread:

  • There is NO published per-school RAN (random number) cutoff for any school. RANs are per-applicant random numbers that only break ties within a priority or screened group. Priority groups are served in order regardless of RAN, and each applicant gets a new RAN for each waitlist. (DOE: Random Numbers in Admissions.) Any website showing a precise “RAN cutoff” for a school is estimating, not quoting the DOE.
  • There is no DOE-published per-school screened-group (SAG) cutoff either. What the DOE publishes is the citywide grade-average thresholds for Fall 2027. These are eligibility benchmarks — being in Group 1 does not guarantee an offer at any particular school:
    Group 1 — average ≥ 94.67Group 2 — ≥ 90.33Group 3 — ≥ 84.00Group 4 — ≥ 77.25
  • NYC SIFT cutoff ranges are model ESTIMATES. If you use NYC SIFT alongside this guide, treat its ranges as informed estimates (labelled “SIFT estimate”), never as official DOE cutoffs.
  • Specialized high schools have NO waitlists. Offers there are by SHSAT or audition score only — there is no “waitlist round” to move in.
  • Demand on each card = applicants per seat, admissions cycle 2024–25 unless a different cycle is noted, from DOE / MySchools data via NYC SIFT / Public School Match. Waitlist offers = the number of offers made from the waitlist in 2023–24 only, where found; where no figure was found, the card honestly says “Not found” rather than guessing.
  • SHSAT lowest qualifying scores are Fall 2026 9th-grade entry ONLY. They are not cut-offs for 2027 — scores move each year with the applicant pool.

Admissions methods, explained

The same school can use different methods for different programs. This is the vocabulary MySchools uses.

SHSAT Test

For the 8 specialized high schools only. Admission is by SHSAT score alone, in rank order of score and your specialized-school preference.

LaGuardia Audition

LaGuardia admits by audition into 6 studios. Separate application and audition process from the general application.

Audition (non-LaGuardia)

Audition is the sole criterion. You submit one common submission per discipline (for example, one vocal submission can serve multiple programs).

Screened

Based on 7th-grade core course grades. Students are placed into Groups 1–4 by grades, and programs admit by group and lottery / ranking within it.

Screened with Assessment

Grades plus an additional component — essay, interview, test or video — weighted by the program. Check each program's exact weighting in MySchools.

Educational Option (Ed. Opt.)

Programs aim for a mix of High, Middle and Low academic levels, with a lottery within each category.

Open

Priority groups first (for example borough or continuing-student priority), then offers by random lottery number (RAN).

Zoned

Guarantee or priority for students who live in the school's zone. Check your zoned school in MySchools.

Language criteria

Some programs consider home language / English-learner status or language proficiency as part of admissions. Details are program-specific in MySchools.
